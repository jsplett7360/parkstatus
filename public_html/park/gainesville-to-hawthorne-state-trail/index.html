About Gainesville-to-Hawthorne State Trail

The Florida state park system consists of 176 units managed by the Florida Park Service (FPS), a division of the Florida Department of Environmental Protection. It encompasses more than 819,000 acres (331,000 ha) distributed across 63 of Florida's 67 counties. This list covers all 176 units currently managed by FPS as well as an undetermined property and former park. The units are grouped into 17 different classifications; state parks, state recreation areas, and state preserves are the most common. A full summary of classification and administrative district can be found in the Parks system summary section. Established in 1935, the system ranges from pre-Columbian archaeological sites to first-magnitude springs, coral reefs, and roadless barrier islands only accessible by boat or ferry.
