Where this comes from

Beach status reflects public monitoring data from the New York State Department of Health and county health departments — Open, Water quality advisory, or Closed to swimming. Some beaches are managed by another agency and show as “no data.” It is our reading of that data, not an official determination, and it refreshes hourly. Always confirm with the operating agency before you swim.
