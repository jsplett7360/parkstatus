About this status board

Every status here is derived from the National Park Service's own alerts and operating-hours data. A park reads open when no active closures or hazards are reported, partially closed when part of it — a trail, road, or area — is closed or under a hazard, and closed when the whole site is closed or outside its scheduled hours. Most parks sit in the middle: a closed trail rarely means a closed park, so check the reason and the official page before you drive out. More in our guides.
