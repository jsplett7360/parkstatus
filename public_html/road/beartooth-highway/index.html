The 68-mile alpine highway from Red Lodge, Montana over 10,947-ft Beartooth Pass to the Northeast Entrance of Yellowstone.

When does Beartooth Highway open?

Typically open late May, around Memorial Day weekend to mid-October (often Columbus Day), earlier with heavy snow. Plowing usually begins in April; the opening date depends entirely on snowpack and can swing several weeks.

A U.S. Forest Service scenic byway, not inside a national park. Montana DOT and Wyoming DOT open and close it; snow can shut sections any month of the year.
