Is Road to Paradise (Longmire–Paradise) open in winter?

Open year-round from the Nisqually Entrance, the only winter way into the park. A gate at Longmire closes every night and reopens each morning once the road above has been plowed and checked for avalanche danger — so the upper road is shut most winter nights and some full days. In a few recent low-staffing winters, access above Longmire was limited to weekends and holidays. Tire chains must be carried in the vehicle from November 1 to May 1.
