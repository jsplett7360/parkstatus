When does Old Fall River Road open?

Typically open the first week of July (late June in a light-snow year) to early-to-mid October. Plowing usually begins in April; the opening date depends entirely on snowpack and can swing several weeks.

One-way uphill, gravel, no trailers or large vehicles. It opens weeks after Trail Ridge Road — the narrow valley holds avalanche debris into June — and closes at the first snowfall. After the vehicle season it reopens briefly to bikes and walkers before full winter closure. From late May to mid-October it sits inside Rocky Mountain National Park's timed-entry reservation zone.

Reservations

Rocky Mountain National Park requires a timed-entry reservation from Friday, May 22 through about October 13, 2026 (the Bear Lake Road corridor runs a few days longer). Two permits are sold on Recreation.gov: “Timed Entry” (all areas except Bear Lake Road, needed 9 a.m.–2 p.m.) and “Timed Entry + Bear Lake Road” (needed 5 a.m.–6 p.m.). Reservations open May 1 for late May/June, then on the 1st of each prior month; 40% of each day’s slots release at 7 p.m. MDT the night before. $2 processing fee. No reservation needed outside those hours or dates.
