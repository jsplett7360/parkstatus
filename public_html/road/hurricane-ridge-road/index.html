Is Hurricane Ridge Road open in winter?

Open daily in summer. From roughly late November through March the road is open Friday, Saturday and Sunday only (plus some holidays), and only when it has been plowed and avalanche conditions allow — check the park's gate status before driving up. Visitor services at the top have been limited since the Hurricane Ridge Day Lodge burned down in May 2023.

How we know this

We watch Olympic National Park's official National Park Service alerts for anything that names Hurricane Ridge Road, and refresh this page with the daily build.
